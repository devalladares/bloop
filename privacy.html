Bloop Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Bloop is a private app for one small group of friends (up to eight people). It has no ads and no analytics.

What we store

Your name, colour and profile picture; the messages, word stickers, photos, Moments, Catch-ups, Imagine pictures, plans and reactions you post; and a device token so Bloop can send you notifications. Sign-in uses Sign in with Apple or Google through Firebase Authentication; Bloop receives only an account identifier and, if you share it, your email address.

Who can see it

Only members of your group. Private chats are visible only to the people in them. Nothing is public.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the asia-south1 (Mumbai) region. Photos have location details removed before upload. Pictures made in Imagine are created on your iPhone with Apple's Image Playground.

Reports and blocking

You can report or block anyone. Reports go to the group admin, who reviews them within 24 hours and removes content or people that break the Rules.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile and the things you posted. Deleting the app alone does not.

Testing

If you use Bloop through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer.

Children

Bloop is not meant for children under 13.
