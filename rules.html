Bloop Rules

Last updated October 11, 2026

Bloop is for friends. By joining you agree to these rules. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people.

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate.
  • No nudity, sexual content or graphic violence.
  • No spam, scams or pretending to be someone else.
  • Only post photos you have the right to share; don't share other people's private information.
  • Nothing illegal.

Every message and post can be reported, and anyone can be blocked. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks these rules is removed, and so are the people who post it.

You own what you post. You can delete it, or your whole account, at any time in the app.
